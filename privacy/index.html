Tasklings: To-Do Buddy — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Tasklings is a to-do list with a friendly on-screen buddy. Short version: your tasks stay on your device unless you turn on Sync. If you do, they're encrypted on your device before they're sent, so we can't read them. We don't collect, sell or share your personal data.

What the app stores, and where

Everything below is stored on your device, inside the app's private storage. Only the items marked “synced” ever leave it, and only if you turn on Sync:

  • Your tasks, reminder times, and finished-task history for today (synced)
  • Your progress: XP, level and streak (synced), and your current NOW task (synced)
  • Your settings: chosen character, theme, nudge and focus-timer preferences, buddy position
  • Photo characters (optional): if you make a character from a photo, the app copies a small cropped version of the picture you choose into its private storage. You pick the photo with Android's photo picker, so the app only sees the one picture you select. It is never uploaded.

We (the developer) cannot see any of this. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Sync (optional, off unless you turn it on)

Sync keeps your list the same on your phones and computers. It's off until you choose “Turn on sync” or “I have a code” in Settings.

  • What is sent: your tasks (titles, done state, reminder times, colours), your NOW task, and your XP and streak. Settings, your chosen character and photo characters are not sent.
  • End-to-end encrypted: your sync code is a random secret created on your device. The app uses it to encrypt everything (AES-256-GCM) before it leaves your device. The server only ever receives scrambled data plus a random ID, and never sees your code, so neither we nor anyone else can read your tasks.
  • Where it's stored: on our sync server at sync.tasklings.download, run on Cloudflare's network. One encrypted copy is kept for your devices to share.
  • What the server sees: like any website, the network connection reveals your IP address and the time of each sync. We don't use these to identify you; Cloudflare may keep them in short-lived operational logs, covered by Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • How long it's kept, and deleting it: until you delete it. Choose Settings → Sync → Turn off → “Erase for all devices” and the encrypted copy is deleted from the server straight away. Turning sync off on just one device leaves the copy for your other devices.
  • Keep your code safe: anyone with your sync code can join your sync, just like a password. If you lose it, we can't recover it.

AI helper (optional, off unless you turn it on)

The AI helper can split a task into small steps (“✨ Break it down”) or turn a brain dump into a list. It's off until you turn it on in Settings, and it only acts when you tap one of those buttons.

  • What is sent: only the text of that one task, or the text you typed for the brain dump. Nothing else (no other tasks, settings or anything about you).
  • Where it goes: through our server at sync.tasklings.download to Google Gemini, which writes the steps or tasks and sends them back. This text is not end-to-end encrypted, because the AI has to read it; it is encrypted in transit.
  • Not stored, not used for training: our server doesn't store or log the text. We use Google's paid Gemini API, under which Google doesn't use it to train its models (see Gemini API terms).
  • Daily limit: to keep costs in check, the app sends a random ID (not linked to you or your tasks) so the server can count uses per device per day.

Backups

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include the app's data in your own device backup (for example, your Google account backup), so your list comes back when you restore or move to a new phone. That backup is managed by Android and Google under your account, not by us. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Purchases

The optional one-time “Unlock everything” purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether the unlock has been bought, so it can switch the extra features on (including after you reinstall). Google's handling of your payment information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Mac app

The Tasklings Mac app works the same way: your tasks are stored in your own user folder on the Mac, and Sync (if you turn it on) sends the same end-to-end encrypted data described above. It checks GitHub for app updates, which reveals your IP address to GitHub.

Children

Tasklings is designed for a general audience aged 13 and over. It contains no ads and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone. You can also clear it any time in Android Settings → Apps → Tasklings → Storage → Clear storage. If you used Sync, delete the server copy first with Settings → Sync → Turn off → “Erase for all devices”. Uninstalling alone leaves the encrypted copy for your other devices.
